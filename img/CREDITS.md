# Image credits

The Hamilton easter egg is built from two freely licensed photographs from
Wikimedia Commons. Both are **CC BY-SA 4.0**, which asks for attribution and
that derivatives carry the same licence, so the two derived sprites below are
offered under CC BY-SA 4.0 as well.

## hamilton-car.png

Derived from **"Ferrari F138 left 2017 Museo Fernando Alonso.jpg"**
by **Morio**, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ferrari_F138_left_2017_Museo_Fernando_Alonso.jpg

Changes: the car was cut out of its gallery background, the tyres were
re-cut as circles, and the result was scaled and colour-reduced.

## hamilton-head.png

Derived from **"Lewis Hamilton 2016 Malaysia 2.jpg"**
by **Morio**, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lewis_Hamilton_2016_Malaysia_2.jpg

Changes: the head was masked out of the surrounding photo and scaled.

## A note on the likeness

Lewis Hamilton is a real person and has not endorsed anything here. This is a
private keepsake running on one device, the way a fan might tape a photo to a
notebook. It is not for publication, sale, or anything suggesting he is
involved. If this app is ever put somewhere public, take the easter egg out.
